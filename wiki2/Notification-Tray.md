## Notification Tray

The Notification Tray is an area of the app menu that stores all of the [notifications](notification.md) that have been sent in the current session.  You can delete notifications individually, or you can clear all of them at once.

The Notification Tray looks like this:

![Notification Tray](images/Notification-Tray/notif_tray.png)

> **In `siteroot`:** open the home menu (the grid icon at the top left) and click **Notifications** at the bottom. Each notification shows its full header and body. The × next to one removes it; **Clear** removes them all. Notifications aren't saved anywhere, so reloading the page empties the tray.
>
> ![The Notification Tray in siteroot](images/siteroot/notification-tray.png)
